In data visualization convention, "Y versus X" means:

Y is plotted on the vertical axis (y-axis)
X is plotted on the horizontal axis (x-axis).

Here is plot() syntax. first param is x-axis.
plt.plot(x_data, y_data, 'r--o', label='Red Dashed with Circles')
